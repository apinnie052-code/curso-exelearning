Final sessions of the unit

Final product

You have finally reached the end of the project!

Let’s recap the steps we followed:

  1. Analysis of the material corresponding to your area of work (group 1, 2, or 3) and search for additional material.
  2. Summary and organization of the essential information.
  3. Creation of an outline/guide to start preparing the final product (oral presentation).
  4. Publication of the summary material on the class's collaborative Padlet (free format, to be chosen by the students).
  5. Oral presentation of the work in class, followed by a debate.
