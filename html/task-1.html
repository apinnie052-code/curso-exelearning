Instructions

The class has been divided into THREE working groups. Each pair will specialize in a specific area.

  • GROUP 1: Women in public spaces
  • GROUP 2: Gender stereotypes
  • GROUP 3: Harassment in all its forms

In this first task, you are asked to work on the material corresponding to your specific area.
